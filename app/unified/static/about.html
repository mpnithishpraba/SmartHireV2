Features it offers

Everything a small hiring team needs for an early-stage screen, split between what recruiters do and what applicants do.

Recruiters

Roles and openings

Create a role from a template or from scratch. Type required and preferred skills, or pull them from a job-description PDF or a CSV. Set scoring weights and test difficulty.

Recruiters

Resume screening

Every resume is scored against the role on skills, experience, education, certificates, location and shift, with the matching evidence quoted and missing details flagged for confirmation.

Applicants

Aptitude test

A timed multiple-choice test drawn from a local question bank at easy, medium or hard difficulty.

Applicants

Coding test

A coding problem run against hidden test cases on this computer, scored by the share of cases passed.

Applicants

Structured interview

Questions can be read aloud. Applicants type or speak their answers, and speech is transcribed locally with Whisper.

Applicants

Identity check

With consent, a webcam capture is compared with a reference photo. It returns match, no match or inconclusive, and is kept separate from the hiring score.

Recruiters

Interview rating

Rate each answer on a 0–4 rubric yourself, or let AI suggest ratings with a reason for each, then adjust and confirm them.

Recruiters

Dashboard and shortlist

Rank applicants by weighted overall score, select the top N or pick by hand, confirm the shortlist, and download a PDF report per applicant.

Functionality

How SmartHire works, step by step. Each applicant moves through the same stages, and every result lands on one record.

  1. Create a role Recruiter

    Define skills, experience, education, location and shift, and decide how much each counts toward the resume score.

  2. Apply to an opening Applicant

    The role appears under Openings. The applicant clicks Apply now, adds a resume PDF or pastes it, and confirms location and shift.

  3. Automatic resume screen Recruiter

    SmartHire scores the resume against the role, shows the evidence for each criterion, and flags anything a recruiter should confirm.

  4. Take the assessments Applicant

    Identity check, aptitude test, coding test and interview, in any order, all saved to the same record.

  5. Review and rate Recruiter

    Rate interview answers by hand or with AI suggestions. The overall score is resume, aptitude, coding and interview, each weighted (30 / 20 / 25 / 25 by default).

  6. Shortlist and report Recruiter

    Select the top applicants, confirm the shortlist, and share PDF reports with the hiring manager.

Where data goes. Roles, resumes, answers and scores are stored in a database on this computer. Speech transcription, the identity check and code runs also happen here, and camera frames are never saved. Only two optional features send text to Groq's AI service: AI interview rating, which sends the questions and answers, and skill extraction from a PDF, which sends the document text. Both are used only when a Groq key is set.

Benefits

Why teams use one record instead of separate tools.

Faster screening

Resumes are scored the moment they arrive, and the top applicants can be shortlisted in one step.

Fair, consistent scoring

Every applicant for a role is measured on the same criteria and weights, with the evidence shown next to each score.

Humans stay in charge

AI ratings are suggestions. Interview scores and shortlists count only after a recruiter confirms them.

Data stays with you

Records live on your own computer, not in a vendor's cloud, which keeps privacy reviews simple.

Low cost

No per-seat or per-candidate fees. The only optional running cost is the Groq AI service.

Clear audit trail

Earlier ratings, confirmations and resets are kept with timestamps, and every applicant has a downloadable report.
